BL.OWN — merged direction

This fuses Ledger's institutional trust-mark (the seal, the "certified" framing) with OwnBlack's directness (a checkmark built into the icon, a name that says exactly what it is). The tagline does the disambiguation work your name needs — it's always paired with the mark on first contact, so nobody's left guessing how to say it.

BL·OWN
Certified BL·OWN
Black-Owned. Verified. Found.

Why the seal: it's literally a stamp — round, embossed, slightly dimensional — so it reads as "certified" before anyone parses the checkmark inside it. It doubles as the verification badge you'll show on every business profile, so the logo and the trust signal are visually the same object.

Why the tagline stays close: "Certified BL·OWN" gives the acronym away immediately, and "Black-Owned. Verified. Found." spells it out in three plain words underneath. Once people know the platform, the tagline can shrink or drop — but every first touchpoint (nav, app icon splash, email footer) should carry it for at least the first year.

Pronunciation note: lean into it rather than fight it — "BLOWN" said out loud (like blown away) is a genuinely good double meaning for a platform about making an impression. Worth testing as a verbal tagline too: "Get BLOWN away."
