About Millbrook Kitchen

Started in 2019 out of a home kitchen, Millbrook now caters events of 20 to 200 across the Lehigh Valley — small-batch Southern cooking, made-to-order, with same-week booking for most party sizes. Founder Dana Ferris grew up cooking alongside her grandmother in Millbrook, NC, and named the business after the town that taught her to cook for a crowd.
